These Are My Must-Watch Movies, Films and Anime

By Samuel Alayon

Dororo

Its a dark anime where a man sacrificed his first born to 7 demon gods to become a rich king of a prosperous land. The show follows the first born as he slays the demon gods to reclaim his body back.

Zoolander 1 & 2

A silly comedy movie about male models. Its filled with stereotipical jokes and has a ridiculous plot.

Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse

If you haven't seen or heard of this, you need to talk to more people. It is a beautifully animated movie in which every single detail was deliberate. The action is great and the plot kept me glued on the screen.
